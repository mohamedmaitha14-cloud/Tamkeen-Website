ABOUT THE PROGRAM

Learn more about this program

Tamkeen’s 120-hour programme combines UAE sustainability priorities with ESG frameworks, carbon-footprint measurement, green technologies, AI and IoT, circular economy, energy efficiency, sustainable supply chains, reporting, governance and a digital sustainability roadmap.

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

Training topics

01 The sustainability agenda: Net Zero 2050 and the Sustainable Development Goals +
  • Explain the main concepts and use cases behind the sustainability agenda: net zero 2050 and the sustainable development goals
  • Select suitable AI methods, data inputs and tools for the task
  • Practise a relevant example and evaluate reliability, privacy and limitations
02 Environmental, social and governance (ESG) standards and frameworks +
  • Understand the goals, terminology and drivers of environmental, social and governance (esg) standards and frameworks
  • Assess a realistic organisational sustainability challenge
  • Propose measurable actions and monitor their outcomes
03 Carbon footprint measurement and the three scopes +
  • Define the data and performance indicators needed for carbon footprint measurement and the three scopes
  • Interpret sample results using an appropriate analytical approach
  • Present conclusions and recommend evidence-based improvements
04 Green technologies and IoT for sustainability +
  • Explain the main concepts and use cases behind green technologies and iot for sustainability
  • Select suitable AI methods, data inputs and tools for the task
  • Practise a relevant example and evaluate reliability, privacy and limitations
05 Data and AI in measuring and reducing impact +
  • Explain the main concepts and use cases behind data and ai in measuring and reducing impact
  • Select suitable AI methods, data inputs and tools for the task
  • Practise a relevant example and evaluate reliability, privacy and limitations
06 The circular economy and digital waste management +
  • Understand the business drivers and technologies involved in the circular economy and digital waste management
  • Plan an implementation scenario including people, process and systems
  • Identify adoption challenges, governance needs and success measures
07 Renewable energy and smart buildings +
  • Understand the essential principles and terminology of renewable energy and smart buildings
  • Explore methods, tools and examples relevant to renewable energy and smart buildings
  • Apply the learning to a realistic workplace scenario and review the results
08 Sustainable supply chains and digital traceability +
  • Explain the main concepts and use cases behind sustainable supply chains and digital traceability
  • Select suitable AI methods, data inputs and tools for the task
  • Practise a relevant example and evaluate reliability, privacy and limitations
09 Sustainability reporting and disclosure frameworks +
  • Explain the main concepts and use cases behind sustainability reporting and disclosure frameworks
  • Select suitable AI methods, data inputs and tools for the task
  • Practise a relevant example and evaluate reliability, privacy and limitations
10 Governance, compliance and climate risks +
  • Identify common threats, risks and warning signs relating to governance, compliance and climate risks
  • Apply prevention, monitoring and response methods to a practical scenario
  • Document findings, controls and recommended corrective actions
11 Sustainable digital transformation: data centres and the digital footprint +
  • Explain the main concepts and use cases behind sustainable digital transformation: data centres and the digital footprint
  • Select suitable AI methods, data inputs and tools for the task
  • Practise a relevant example and evaluate reliability, privacy and limitations
12 A project building a digital sustainability roadmap for an organisation +
  • Identify the scope, requirements and success measures for a project building a digital sustainability roadmap for an organisation
  • Work through a realistic project scenario using appropriate tools
  • Review deliverables, performance indicators and improvement actions
LEARNING GOALS

Program objectives

  • Understand the UAE Net Zero 2050 agenda and ESG frameworks.
  • Measure carbon footprints across scopes one, two and three.
  • Apply data, IoT and green technology to reduce environmental impact.
  • Understand circular economy and sustainable supply chains.
  • Prepare sustainability reports and governance controls.
  • Build a digital sustainability roadmap.
WHO SHOULD JOIN

Who is it for?

  • Sustainability and environment officers
  • Digital transformation and IT managers
  • Facilities, energy and supply chain professionals
  • Governance and compliance officers
  • Engineers and business graduates

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from digital transformation in business? +

This programme focuses on sustainability impacts, carbon measurement, ESG and green technologies rather than general business process digitalisation.

Does it cover ESG? +

Yes. The course syllabus includes ESG frameworks and organisational assessment.

Does it teach carbon-footprint calculation? +

Yes. It includes the three emissions scopes and a calculation exercise.

Is an engineering background necessary? +

No. the course information says it suits administrative, financial and technical participants.

What is the practical project? +

A three-year digital sustainability roadmap for an organisation.

How long is the programme? +

the course information specifies 120 training hours.

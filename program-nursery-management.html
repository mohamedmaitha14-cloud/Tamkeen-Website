GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Does this cover UAE nursery licensing? +

Yes. The curriculum addresses licensing requirements, approvals and renewals; applications must still follow the relevant emirate authority.

How long is the training? +

100 training hours.

Does it cover nursery finances? +

Yes, including pricing, occupancy, break-even and cash flow.

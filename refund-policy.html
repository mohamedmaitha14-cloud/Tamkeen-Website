PAYMENTS & REFUNDS

Refund & Returns Policy.

Information about payment methods, cancellations, refunds, and transaction support at Tamkeen Training Center.

Payment and Refund Policy

01

Approved Payment Gateways

At Tamkeen Training Center, we provide a secure and convenient payment experience through the following approved payment gateways:

  • Network (Network International)
  • Tabby for buy now, pay later or installment payments

All payments are secured in accordance with international PCI DSS security standards to protect your financial and banking information.

02

Available Payment Methods

You can pay using:

  • Credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, and Mada)
  • Installment or deferred payments through Tabby, subject to Tabby's terms and conditions

All payments are processed in UAE dirhams (AED), unless otherwise stated on the service page.

03

Order Confirmation

After successful payment, you will receive a confirmation email containing your transaction details.

If you do not receive confirmation within 24 hours of payment, please contact us at the email address listed in the Contact Us section below.

04

Cancellation and Refund Policy

Training Services and Courses

Once you have registered for a training course or have been granted access to educational content, the amount paid is non-refundable, except where a technical issue prevents you from accessing the service. In such cases, you must contact us within 3 days of the payment date .

Cancellation Before the Course Begins

If you wish to cancel your registration at least 48 hours before the course start date , a full refund will be issued within a maximum of 7 business days .

Payments Through Tabby

All installment and deferred payments through Tabby are processed directly by Tabby and are subject to that company's terms and conditions. See Tabby's policies .

05

Failed Transactions or Duplicate Charges

If a payment error occurs, or your account is charged without receiving an order confirmation, please contact us immediately and provide the bank transaction reference number.

The transaction will be reviewed and processed within 3–5 business days .

06

Contact Us

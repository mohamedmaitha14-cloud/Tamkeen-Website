ABOUT THE PROGRAM

Learn more about this program

Tamkeen’s 100-hour programme addresses hospital operations, patient flow, bed and capacity management, emergency demand forecasting, workforce scheduling, financial management, inventory, appointments, health information systems, governance and an operational improvement project.

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

Training topics

01 Healthcare facility management in the AI age +
  • Explain the main concepts and use cases behind healthcare facility management in the ai age
  • Select suitable AI methods, data inputs and tools for the task
  • Practise a relevant example and evaluate reliability, privacy and limitations
02 Operations and patient flow management +
  • Explain the key processes and standards involved in operations and patient flow management
  • Apply the concepts to a healthcare service or operational case
  • Identify quality indicators and opportunities for improvement
03 Bed, capacity and occupancy management +
  • Understand the essential principles and terminology of bed, capacity and occupancy management
  • Explore methods, tools and examples relevant to bed, capacity and occupancy management
  • Apply the learning to a realistic workplace scenario and review the results
04 Emergency, outpatient and demand forecasting +
  • Explain the key processes and standards involved in emergency, outpatient and demand forecasting
  • Apply the concepts to a healthcare service or operational case
  • Identify quality indicators and opportunities for improvement
05 Health human resource management and scheduling +
  • Explain the key processes and standards involved in health human resource management and scheduling
  • Apply the concepts to a healthcare service or operational case
  • Identify quality indicators and opportunities for improvement
06 Financial management, revenue and costs +
  • Understand the essential principles and terminology of financial management, revenue and costs
  • Explore methods, tools and examples relevant to financial management, revenue and costs
  • Apply the learning to a realistic workplace scenario and review the results
07 Supplies, medicine and inventory management +
  • Understand the essential principles and terminology of supplies, medicine and inventory management
  • Explore methods, tools and examples relevant to supplies, medicine and inventory management
  • Apply the learning to a realistic workplace scenario and review the results
08 Appointments and administrative patient experience +
  • Explain the key processes and standards involved in appointments and administrative patient experience
  • Apply the concepts to a healthcare service or operational case
  • Identify quality indicators and opportunities for improvement
09 Health information systems and data +
  • Define the data and performance indicators needed for health information systems and data
  • Interpret sample results using an appropriate analytical approach
  • Present conclusions and recommend evidence-based improvements
10 Performance analysis, planning and future capacity +
  • Define the data and performance indicators needed for performance analysis, planning and future capacity
  • Interpret sample results using an appropriate analytical approach
  • Present conclusions and recommend evidence-based improvements
11 Governance, compliance and health standards +
  • Explain the key processes and standards involved in governance, compliance and health standards
  • Apply the concepts to a healthcare service or operational case
  • Identify quality indicators and opportunities for improvement
12 A project improving a healthcare facility’s management +
  • Identify the scope, requirements and success measures for a project improving a healthcare facility’s management
  • Work through a realistic project scenario using appropriate tools
  • Review deliverables, performance indicators and improvement actions
LEARNING GOALS

Program objectives

  • Analyse patient flow and hospital bottlenecks.
  • Forecast bed occupancy and emergency demand.
  • Plan healthcare workforce and resource allocation.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How does this differ from healthcare quality management? +

This programme covers overall hospital operations, capacity, staffing, finance and patient flow; the quality programme specialises in patient safety and accreditation.

Does it cover bed and patient flow management? +

Yes. Dedicated modules address occupancy, capacity and patient flow.

Is the course clinical or administrative? +

The published programme focuses on healthcare facility administration and operations.

Does it cover emergency demand? +

Yes. Demand forecasting for emergency and outpatient services is included.

How long is the programme? +

the course information specifies 100 training hours.

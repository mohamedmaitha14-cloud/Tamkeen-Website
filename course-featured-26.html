ABOUT THE PROGRAM

Learn more about this program

Tamkeen’s 120-hour programme covers AI-assisted procurement, spend analysis, demand forecasting, supplier evaluation, e-sourcing, data-driven negotiation, contract analysis, supply-chain risks, sustainable procurement, governance and a procurement transformation project.

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

Training topics

01 The procurement foundation, cycle and AI’s role +
  • Explain the main concepts and use cases behind the procurement foundation, cycle and ai’s role
  • Select suitable AI methods, data inputs and tools for the task
  • Practise a relevant example and evaluate reliability, privacy and limitations
02 Spend analysis and classification with AI +
  • Explain the main concepts and use cases behind spend analysis and classification with ai
  • Select suitable AI methods, data inputs and tools for the task
  • Practise a relevant example and evaluate reliability, privacy and limitations
03 Demand management, planning and forecasting +
  • Understand the essential principles and terminology of demand management, planning and forecasting
  • Explore methods, tools and examples relevant to demand management, planning and forecasting
  • Apply the learning to a realistic workplace scenario and review the results
04 Supplier selection and evaluation +
  • Map the key stages and responsibilities for supplier selection and evaluation
  • Compare practical approaches using a supplier or purchasing scenario
  • Evaluate cost, compliance, performance and improvement opportunities
05 E-sourcing and smart tenders +
  • Map the key stages and responsibilities for e-sourcing and smart tenders
  • Compare practical approaches using a supplier or purchasing scenario
  • Evaluate cost, compliance, performance and improvement opportunities
06 Data-driven negotiation +
  • Define the data and performance indicators needed for data-driven negotiation
  • Interpret sample results using an appropriate analytical approach
  • Present conclusions and recommend evidence-based improvements
07 Contract management and analysis with AI +
  • Explain the main concepts and use cases behind contract management and analysis with ai
  • Select suitable AI methods, data inputs and tools for the task
  • Practise a relevant example and evaluate reliability, privacy and limitations
08 Supply chain and inventory +
  • Explain the main concepts and use cases behind supply chain and inventory
  • Select suitable AI methods, data inputs and tools for the task
  • Practise a relevant example and evaluate reliability, privacy and limitations
09 Supply and supplier risk +
  • Identify common threats, risks and warning signs relating to supply and supplier risk
  • Apply prevention, monitoring and response methods to a practical scenario
  • Document findings, controls and recommended corrective actions
10 Sustainable and responsible procurement +
  • Explain the main concepts and use cases behind sustainable and responsible procurement
  • Select suitable AI methods, data inputs and tools for the task
  • Practise a relevant example and evaluate reliability, privacy and limitations
11 Compliance, governance and ethics +
  • Understand the essential principles and terminology of compliance, governance and ethics
  • Explore methods, tools and examples relevant to compliance, governance and ethics
  • Apply the learning to a realistic workplace scenario and review the results
12 Automation and a procurement transformation project +
  • Identify the scope, requirements and success measures for automation and a procurement transformation project
  • Work through a realistic project scenario using appropriate tools
  • Review deliverables, performance indicators and improvement actions
LEARNING GOALS

Program objectives

  • Analyse procurement spend and identify savings.
  • Forecast demand and evaluate suppliers using data.
  • Use market analysis to support negotiation.
  • Analyse contracts, inventory and supply risks.
  • Apply sustainable procurement and governance principles.
  • Design a procurement transformation plan.

  • Warehouse and inventory officers
